Nvidia-led 6.7% Nasdaq-100 rebound on 23 Nov; headlines show a premarket fade

What happened

Per the Cboe Daily Take 'The Market Taketh and the Market Giveth Back' (23 Nov), Nvidia's announcement that Jensen Huang was back as CEO set off a broad rally.

Nvidia closed nearly 31.5% higher, its best day since 2003, and the Nasdaq 100 gained 6.7%, the Nasdaq Composite 6.5%, the S&P 500 4.7% (to 7,237) and the Dow 3%. The S&P 500 and Dow are out of correction territory, while the Nasdaq Composite is still about 13% below its October peak. The Cboe Macro Volatility Digest (week of 23 Nov) says Friday's VIX jump was driven by spot selling and higher fixed-strike vols rather than downside-put demand, and that QQQ 1M implied vol jumped over 20 pts last week. This morning's headlines (unverified) describe Nasdaq futures fading after the Nvidia rally, with Nvidia down in premarket. The inputs contain no live prices, so this brief does not say where NQ trades.

Dominant driver

Single-stock or sector news

Cboe attributes the 23 Nov rally to one Nvidia headline (Huang's return, confirmed by the 8-K) and says sentiment trumped fundamentals. The AI complex, not macro data or rates, is setting the direction of index and volatility moves.

Leader

QQQ

Per Cboe, the Nasdaq 100 rose 6.7% against 4.7% for the S&P 500 on 23 Nov, and VXN minus VIX is 9.69 pts (F44), so tech carries the extra risk premium. The FRED-based Nasdaq-100 minus S&P 500 gap (-0.19 pts last session, -0.78 over 5 sessions) is stale to 19 Nov and predates the rebound. COR1M fell to 25.68 from 36.05, which fits the Digest's picture of an AI-specific rotation rather than a broad macro move.

Market
Cboe volatility fell sharply on 23 Nov: VIX 26.62 (-25.83%), VXN 36.31 (-19.76%), VVIX 117.36 (-14.98%), VIX9D 27.76 (-31.83%) and COR1M 25.68 (-28.77%). SKEW rose 1.10% to 140.37. Headlines from Kitco, Moomoo, CNBC and Barchart (unverified) describe futures turning lower after the rally; one gives a 1.7% futures decline, and a Yahoo Finance headline gives a 5% Nvidia premarket fall.
Rates and macro
Treasury (official) 23 Nov: 2-year 4.63% (+0.0 bp), 10-year 4.92% (+0.0 bp), 30-year 5.29% (-2.0 bp), 3-month 4.37% (+5.0 bp), so yields did not rebound with equities. FRED 10y breakeven was 2.13% on 23 Nov and the 10y real yield 2.77% on 20 Nov. HY OAS was 3.76 on 20 Nov (+24.0 bp), the widest in the table and not yet updated for the rally. Polymarket (not CME FedWatch) prices a 25 bp hike in December at 93.5% and 50+ bp at 3.3%. BLS October data: CPI +0.35% m/m, core CPI +0.3%, unemployment 4.1%, payrolls +165.0k.

Companies

Cboe says Nvidia options volume ran at roughly three times normal and that Anthropic options already price a $19 implied move to the 18 Dec expiration. CrowdStrike fell nearly 9% and Zscaler rose nearly 18% on the $43.9 billion all-stock deal (Cboe). A META 8-K (primary source) says it will not issue Class C shares while the recapitalization litigation is pending.
