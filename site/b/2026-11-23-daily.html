VIX near 36 after Friday's AI rout; Nasdaq-100 near bear-market territory per Cboe

What happened

The Cboe Daily Take 'Market Selloff Deepens as Investors Seek Shelter' (20 Nov) says Friday did not look like dip-buying.

The S&P 500 and Dow closed in correction territory, the S&P 500 broke below 7,000, and the Nasdaq Composite fell 5.3%. Cboe says the Nasdaq 100 was dangerously close to a bear market (a 20% or larger decline). Cboe volatility surged: VIX 35.89 (+26.20%), its highest since April 2025 per Cboe, VXN 45.25 and COR1M 36.05. Treasury yields fell sharply as money sought shelter. Unverified headlines this morning describe a futures bounce. The inputs have no live prices, so this brief does not say where NQ trades.

Dominant driver

Single-stock or sector news

Cboe ties Friday's leg lower to the Nvidia SEC-probe reports, the Anthropic post-IPO slide and the wider AI selloff. Volatility and positioning are amplifying it (VIX 35.89, VIX9D 40.72, COR1M 36.05), but the trigger was still AI and chip-sector news; rates eased.

Leader

QQQ

Nasdaq-100 minus S&P 500 was -0.19 pct pts on 19 Nov and -0.78 over 5 sessions, and VXN minus VIX is 9.36 pts, so Nasdaq-100 vol still carries a premium. COR1M at 36.05 shows the selling is broadening, but Cboe still places tech and semiconductors at the centre, and the Nasdaq Composite's 5.3% drop on Friday was far larger than the S&P 500 move Cboe describes.
