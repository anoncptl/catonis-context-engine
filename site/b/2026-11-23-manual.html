AI rout: VIX 35.89; Huang reinstated at Nvidia per 8-K; bounce is unverified

What happened

The Cboe Daily Take 'Market Selloff Deepens as Investors Seek Shelter' (20 Nov) says Friday looked like an escape to protection, not dip-buying.

The S&P 500 and Dow closed in correction territory, the Nasdaq Composite fell 5.3%, and the Nasdaq 100 was 'dangerously close' to a bear market. Cboe says Nvidia lost nearly 14% on reports of an SEC probe into its accounting and Anthropic (ANT) lost more than 9%. Over the weekend, an NVDA 8-K (SEC filing, a primary source) says the board reinstated Jen-Hsun Huang as President and CEO effective immediately. Several headlines this morning describe a futures bounce, but they are unverified and the inputs carry no live prices.

Dominant driver

Single-stock or sector news

The AI unwind is Nvidia-centred: SEC-probe reports on Friday, then a CEO reversal in the 8-K. ANT and AMD fell in the same selloff, and the move is amplified by volatility (VIX 35.89, COR1M 36.05) and a priced December hike.
