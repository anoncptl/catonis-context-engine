The hike path is nearly fully priced by Polymarket (25 bps at 87.5%, 50+ bps at 9.2%), and the 2-year, 10-year and 30-year yields all rose on 13 Nov. Tech fell harder than the broad index on a day when yields rose, which fits a rate-sensitive move (engine read). NVDA is an overlay, not the main driver.
