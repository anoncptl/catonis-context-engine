Nvidia earnings after the close; the AI selloff hit a 2.67% Nasdaq-100 drop on 16 Nov

What happened

Per the Cboe Daily Take 'Nvidia Tugs Market Lower Again' (16 Nov), Nvidia fell about another 4% and the AI and semiconductor complex led the market lower, with selling spreading beyond tech.

FRED shows the Nasdaq-100 closed at 27197.15 (-2.67%) and the S&P 500 at 7416.59 (-1.92%). Volatility jumped: VIX 22.91, VXN 32.9, VVIX 114.23 and COR1M 16.42 (+39.15%). Yields eased (10-year 5.12%, -3.0 bp), so this was an AI and equity move rather than a rates shock. Nvidia reports after the US close today, its first call since the CEO resignation, and the Cboe Macro Volatility Digest (week of 16 Nov) says options imply an 8% one-day move.

Dominant driver

Single-stock or sector news

The Cboe Daily Take attributes the 16 Nov selloff mainly to Nvidia and the AI trade, while yields fell and so did not drive the move. Nvidia's results and guidance after the close are the next large catalyst.

Leader

QQQ

The Nasdaq-100 underperformed the S&P 500 by 0.75 pct pts on the last session and by 0.78 pct pts over five sessions. VXN is 9.99 pts above VIX, so tech carries the stress. Engine read: QQQ leads in the sense that Nasdaq-100 weakness is setting the market's direction; COR1M rising to 16.42 hints at some spread beyond tech.

Companies
Cboe says Nvidia is being hit by the leadership transition and by doubts over the sustainability of AI spending. Options imply an 8.5% move (Daily Take) or 8% (Digest), against an average realized move of 4.8% over the past four quarters. The Digest says put buying was 32% of Nvidia retail opening trades on Cboe on Thursday, vs a 21% year-to-date average. Disney (9% implied move) and Home Depot (just under 5%) also report, per the Daily Take.
