Nasdaq-100 -2.67% on Nvidia and AI selling; yields eased; NVDA earnings after close

What happened

The Cboe Daily Take 'Nvidia Tugs Market Lower Again' (16 Nov) says Nvidia fell about another 4% and the AI and semiconductor complex led the market lower.

FRED shows the Nasdaq-100 closed at 27197.15 (-2.67%) and the S&P 500 at 7416.59 (-1.92%). Cboe volatility jumped: VIX 22.91, VXN 32.9, VVIX 114.23 and COR1M 16.42 (+39.15%). Treasury yields eased on the day, so this was an equity and AI-driven move rather than a rates shock. The next catalyst is Nvidia's first earnings report since the CEO resignation, due after the US close on Tuesday.

Dominant driver

Single-stock or sector news

The Nasdaq-100 fell 2.67% while Treasury yields eased (10-year -3.0 bp), so rates did not drive Monday's selling. The Cboe Daily Take attributes the decline to Nvidia and the AI trade, and the next test is Nvidia's earnings and guidance.

Leader

QQQ

The Nasdaq-100 minus the S&P 500 was -0.75 pct pts on the last session and -0.78 pct pts over five sessions, so tech is leading the declines. VXN minus VIX is 9.99 pts, and COR1M rose to 16.42, so correlation is rising but the move is still tech-led.

Detail

Market
Per FRED, the Nasdaq-100 fell 2.67% and the S&P 500 1.92%. The Cboe Daily Take says the Nasdaq Composite fell 2.5% and the Dow 1.4%, and that selling spread beyond tech, with money moving into staples such as Procter & Gamble, Costco and Colgate. VIX9D rose 29.91% to 23.15, so short-dated hedging demand grew fastest. The Daily Take quotes a VIX of 23.56, while the Cboe index data in the inputs show 22.91; this brief uses the 22.91 figure.
Rates and macro
Official Treasury data show the 2-year at 4.83% (-5.0 bp), the 10-year at 5.12% (-3.0 bp) and the 30-year at 5.46% (-1.0 bp). The latest 10-year real yield is 2.91% (FRED, as of 13 Nov, +5.0 bp). Polymarket (prediction-market prices, not CME FedWatch) puts a 25 bps December hike at 91.5% and a 50+ bps hike at 5.1%. The Cboe Macro Volatility Digest (16 Nov) says a December hike is fully priced and the OIS market assigns about 7% to a 50 bps hike. October CPI was +0.35% m/m and core CPI +0.3% m/m (BLS). The inputs contain no content from the Warsh speech on Monday.
Geopolitics
FRED oil data are stale (as of 11 Nov: Brent 92.14, WTI 89.62). The Cboe Daily Take says WTI fell 3.4% to $85.57 on reports of recovering Strait of Hormuz flows and a forecast fourth-quarter supply surplus, despite fresh tanker incidents. One headline (unverified, WSJ) says oil futures slid after Trump said new strikes on Iran are unlikely.
Companies
Nvidia reports after the close on Tuesday. The Cboe Daily Take says options price an 8.5% implied move, and the Macro Volatility Digest says 8% against an average realized move of 4.8% over the past four quarters. The Digest also says retail put buying made up 32% of NVDA opening trades on Thursday versus a 21% year-to-date average. Disney (9% implied move) is also reporting, and Home Depot is reporting as well (implied move just under 5%), per the Daily Take. Headlines (unverified) say AMD, Micron and Nvidia led the AI trade lower and that futures were mixed to softer ahead of Nvidia and the Anthropic IPO.
