Anthropic debut triggers broad selloff; VIX near 30, COR1M jumps to 30.22

What happened

Per the Cboe Daily Take 'Anthropic Hits the Market, but Wall Street Hits the Brakes' (19 Nov), the market rallied briefly on the Anthropic IPO and then rolled over within minutes in what looked like a sell-the-news reaction.

The Dow, S&P 500 and Nasdaq Composite all closed down more than 2% just above their session lows, and technology and semiconductors were the biggest culprits. FRED shows the Nasdaq-100 closed at 26517.05 (-2.21%). Cboe says all three indices are now down more than 3% in November, the first such November since 2008 at this point in the month. Volatility jumped: VIX 28.44 (+15.80%), VXN 36.48 and COR1M 30.22 (+30.03%).

Dominant driver

Single-stock or sector news

Cboe frames the session as an AI-complex sell-the-news reaction to the Anthropic IPO, with the AMD allegations adding to tech and semiconductor pressure. The volatility jump (VIX 28.44, COR1M 30.22) shows the selling widened, so volatility and positioning is an amplifier. Yields fell, which argues against rates as the main cause.

Leader

QQQ

VXN minus VIX is 8.04 pts, so Nasdaq-100 vol is still priced above S&P vol, and Cboe names tech and semiconductors as the biggest culprits. The last FRED-based facts are stale (Nasdaq-100 minus S&P 500 +0.65 pct pts on 18 Nov, -0.55 over five sessions), but the 5-session figure shows tech lagging. COR1M at 30.22 (+30.03%) suggests the selling broadened, so leadership is less clear-cut than before.

Detail

Market
Cboe says the selling reached nearly every corner of the market. Consumer discretionary, communication services, healthcare and industrials each lost 2% or more. The FRED S&P 500 series only runs to 18 Nov (7323.63, -0.23%), so a code-computed S&P 19 Nov change is not available. Cboe puts the AMD drop at 7.7%. Anthropic closed 17% above $83 per Cboe. Headlines (unverified) describe tech futures extending the slide this morning and Anthropic giving back gains on day two. The inputs have no live prices, so this brief does not say where NQ trades.
Rates and macro
Yields fell on the day, so this was not a rates shock: Treasury 10-year 5.04% (-3.0 bp), 2-year 4.76% (-4.0 bp), 30-year 5.4% (-1.0 bp), and 10-year breakeven 2.16%. HY OAS was 3.47 (+4.0 bp) as of 18 Nov, wider on each of the last four observations. Polymarket (prediction-market prices, not CME FedWatch) shows 92.5% for a 25 bps December hike and 3.8% for 50+ bps. Cboe cites CME FedWatch at 100% for a hike of at least 25 bps. The latest official data show October CPI +0.35% m/m (core +0.3%), unemployment 4.1% and payrolls +165.0k.
Geopolitics
Cboe lists ongoing US-Iran tensions as one source of unease. Headlines (unverified) mention Iranian tanker attacks near Hormuz alongside slipping oil prices. FRED oil data are stale to 17 Nov (Brent 92.94, WTI 89.53), so there is no confirmed oil move for 19 Nov.
Companies
Per Cboe, Anthropic (ANT) raised a record $81.3 billion and opened at a 34% premium to its $83 offer price. AMD fell after a short-seller alleged ties to a China-based company that could violate export restrictions, and a newsletter questioned circular financing; Cboe says AMD denied both. Cboe also flags growing worry about AI costs and chipmaker China relationships. Nvidia succession and probe questions from earlier Cboe posts remain open. Headlines (unverified) describe AI stocks extending the slide.
