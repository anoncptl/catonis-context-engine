Rates repricing and NVDA CEO exit hit Nasdaq-100 (-1.22%) as hike bets build

What happened

The Nasdaq-100 closed Fri 13 Nov at 27943.74 (-1.22%) and the S&P 500 at 7561.67 (-0.40%), so tech again fell more than the broad index.

The Treasury curve sold off: the 10-year closed at 5.15% (+5.0 bp) and the 2-year at 4.88% (+2.0 bp). Polymarket (prediction-market prices, not CME FedWatch) puts a 25 bps December hike at 86.5% and a 50+ bps hike at 9.2%. An NVIDIA 8-K (primary source) says CEO Jen-Hsun Huang resigned effective immediately, with CFO Colette Kress as interim CEO. Volatility rose on the day: VIX 19.3, VXN 29.38, VVIX 101.53.

Dominant driver

Fed and rates

The 2-year, 10-year and 30-year yields all rose on 13 Nov (+2.0, +5.0, +4.0 bp), the 10-year real yield is up sharply since 9 Nov, and Polymarket prices a 25 bps December hike at 86.5% with a further 9.2% on a 50+ bps hike. The NVDA CEO exit is a large single-stock overlay on the Nasdaq-100, but rates are the common backdrop across the last two weeks.

Leader

QQQ

The Nasdaq-100 minus S&P 500 was -0.82 pct pts last session, with VXN minus VIX at 10.08 pts, so Nasdaq-100 volatility is priced well above the S&P's. The 5-session gap is +1.03 pct pts, so QQQ led the rebound and then the pullback; it is the higher-beta index in both directions. COR1M at 11.8 is low, which suggests stock-specific moves rather than a broad market move.
