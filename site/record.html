How to read this

Every brief makes three forecasts on one number: the Nasdaq-100 change from a reference close to the close 1, 5 or 21 sessions later, split into bear, base and bull by two cut points. Code grades them from FRED closes.

Brier score: 0 is perfect, 2 is the worst. Equal odds (one third each) always score 0.667. "History" uses how often each outcome happened in the past with VIX in the same range. The engine is only useful if its score is below both.
