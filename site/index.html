What happened

Nvidia-led 6.7% Nasdaq-100 rebound on 23 Nov; headlines show a premarket fade

Per the Cboe Daily Take 'The Market Taketh and the Market Giveth Back' (23 Nov), Nvidia's announcement that Jensen Huang was back as CEO set off a broad rally. Nvidia closed nearly 31.5% higher, its best day since 2003, and the Nasdaq 100 gained 6.7%, the Nasdaq Composite 6.5%, the S&P 500 4.7% (to 7,237) and the Dow 3%. The S&P 500 and Dow are out of correction territory, while the Nasdaq Composite is still about 13% below its October peak. The Cboe Macro Volatility Digest (week of 23 Nov) says Friday's VIX jump was driven by spot selling and higher fixed-strike vols rather than downside-put demand, and that QQQ 1M implied vol jumped over 20 pts last week. This morning's headlines (unverified) describe Nasdaq futures fading after the Nvidia rally, with Nvidia down in premarket. The inputs contain no live prices, so this brief does not say where NQ trades.
