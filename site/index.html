Dip buyers rescue a brutal AI selloff; Nasdaq-100 -0.64%, US shut today

What happened

The Cboe Daily Take 'Call It Wall Street's Turkey of a Trading Day' (25 Nov) says stocks opened with a deep selloff led by Nvidia, then dip buyers pulled them well off the lows.

The Nasdaq-100 briefly touched bear-market territory intraday, and the S&P 500 fell as much as 3.21% and went below 7,000 before closing 0.35% lower, per Cboe. FRED shows the Nasdaq-100 closed at 26046.9 (-0.64%). Cboe names hotter inflation data, renewed tariff concerns and Nvidia credit-rating actions as pressures, with defensive rotation and a bond rally limiting losses. US markets are closed today for the holiday, and Cboe warns Friday's half-day session will be thin and can exaggerate moves.

Detail

Market
Per Cboe, the Nasdaq Composite closed -0.62% after an intraday drop of up to 4.7%, the Nasdaq-100 -0.64%, the S&P 500 -0.35% and the Dow +0.33%. Nvidia fell 3.9% and Anthropic rose 1.08% after an early drop of nearly 10%. VIX closed at 28.29 (+7.32%), VXN 38.53 (+5.79%), VVIX 115.38, SKEW 142.06, VIX9D 29.61 (+10.98%) and COR1M 25.45 (+3.37%). Cboe says options implied a daily S&P 500 move of about 1.8%.
Rates and macro
Per Cboe, core PCE rose 3.3% y/y from 3.1% in September and headline PCE 3.8%. FRED shows core PCE at 0.29% m/m and 3.26% y/y, and headline PCE at 0.32% m/m and 3.84% y/y. Unofficial consensus for core PCE m/m was 0.3%, so the m/m print looks in line while Cboe calls the inflation data hotter than expected. Treasury yields fell: 2-year 4.61% (-2.0 bp), 10-year 4.85% (-6.0 bp), 30-year 5.24% (-6.0 bp). Polymarket gives a 25 bp December hike 93.5%. Cboe cites CME FedWatch at 59% for another 25 bp in January, down from 70% a week earlier. FRED high-yield spread was 3.72 on 24 Nov, so it is a day stale.
Geopolitics
Not covered by the sources apart from unverified headlines, which conflict on oil (one says prices rebound on Middle East supply fears, another that they fall on Gulf attacks and a surplus). Official oil data is stale to 24 Nov: Brent 91.8, WTI 82.24.
Companies
Per Cboe, S&P Global Ratings put Nvidia's senior unsecured debt on CreditWatch negative over management and governance, citing board independence and key-man risk. Moody's lowered the outlook from positive to stable last week. Cboe notes this could eventually influence Nvidia's circular financing with other AI firms. A headline (unverified) claims an Nvidia shipment halt, and another says AI stocks resumed their slide. Both are unconfirmed.
