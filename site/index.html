What happened

Anthropic debut triggers broad selloff; VIX near 30, COR1M jumps to 30.22

Per the Cboe Daily Take 'Anthropic Hits the Market, but Wall Street Hits the Brakes' (19 Nov), the market rallied briefly on the Anthropic IPO and then rolled over within minutes in what looked like a sell-the-news reaction. The Dow, S&P 500 and Nasdaq Composite all closed down more than 2% just above their session lows, and technology and semiconductors were the biggest culprits. FRED shows the Nasdaq-100 closed at 26517.05 (-2.21%). Cboe says all three indices are now down more than 3% in November, the first such November since 2008 at this point in the month. Volatility jumped: VIX 28.44 (+15.80%), VXN 36.48 and COR1M 30.22 (+30.03%).
