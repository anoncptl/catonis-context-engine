What happened

Hawkish Fed minutes, Nvidia fades again, Anthropic debuts today; tech vol still high

Per the Cboe Daily Take 'Nasdaq Breaks Losing Streak as Dow and S&P Slide Again' (18 Nov), Nvidia opened higher after earnings but closed nearly 3% lower, while the Nasdaq Composite snapped a four-session losing streak. Unverified headlines this morning describe Nasdaq futures rising ahead of Anthropic's debut, and others say yields are edging higher. The inputs contain no live prices, so this brief does not say where NQ trades.
